Southwest National Parks Road Trip

November 21–27, 2026 · 7 days / 6 nights · Round-trip flights via Las Vegas (LAS)

A focused week through northern Arizona and southern Utah: Grand Canyon South Rim, Monument Valley, a single Horseshoe Bend stop in Page, Bryce Canyon, and Zion. The route keeps all six booked nights, removes the Page-area tour and Four Corners detour, and finishes with a half day in Zion followed by an overnight in Las Vegas before the flight home.

  • Booked: Ramada by Wyndham Williams, Nov 21–22 ($99.22); Grand Canyon Inn, Nov 22–23 ($140.83); Hampton Inn Kayenta, Nov 23–24 ($147.41); Bryce area, Nov 24–25 ($96.30); Springdale, Nov 25–26 ($206.91); Las Vegas, Nov 26–27 ($127.71).
  • Rental car: Booked at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), pickup Nov 21 at 3:00 PM and return Nov 27 at 12:00 PM ($444.70).
  • Page: Stop at Horseshoe Bend on the drive to Bryce; allow 60–90 minutes. No Antelope Canyon tour or overnight in Page.
  • Flight: Return from Las Vegas on Nov 27; aim for a departure at 3 PM or later.

Day-by-day itinerary

Drive times are estimates before meal, fuel, sightseeing, and weather stops. Late November has short daylight, and Bryce can have winter road or trail conditions.

Day 1 — Saturday, November 21

Las Vegas → Williams

Allow about 3.5–4 hours of driving after rental pickup.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Las Vegas → Williams

  • Arrive in Las Vegas before noon, collect the rental car, and pick up groceries, water, and snacks.
  • Drive via US-93 and I-40 to Williams. Take breaks and avoid adding sightseeing if the flight or rental pickup runs late.
  • Stay at the Ramada by Wyndham Williams/Grand Canyon Area. Booked for Nov 21–22 · $99.22.

Day 2 — Sunday, November 22

Williams → Grand Canyon South Rim → Valle

About 1 hour to the South Rim, then roughly 45 minutes to Valle, plus time in the park.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Williams → Grand Canyon South Rim → Valle

  • Spend the day on the South Rim: Mather Point, the Rim Trail, and a few viewpoints that fit your pace and daylight.
  • Keep the walk mostly along the rim; do not start a strenuous canyon descent late in the day.
  • Stay at the Grand Canyon Inn in Valle. Booked for Nov 22–23 · $140.83.

Day 3 — Monday, November 23

Valle → Desert View → Monument Valley → Kayenta

Plan about 4–5 hours of driving, before stops and any guided tour.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Valle → Grand Canyon → Monument Valley

  • Drive east along Desert View Drive and stop at accessible overlooks, including Desert View if conditions and timing allow.
  • Continue to Monument Valley for the main viewpoints. A guided Navajo tour is optional; check the operator's seasonal hours and road conditions before booking.
  • Stay at the Hampton Inn Kayenta. Booked for Nov 23–24 · $147.41.

Day 4 — Tuesday, November 24

Kayenta → Horseshoe Bend → Bryce Canyon City

Google Maps estimates about 4 hr 16 min / 252 miles of driving, before the stop and breaks.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Kayenta → Horseshoe Bend → Bryce Canyon City

  • Start early enough to reach Bryce before dark. Keep the Page stop to Horseshoe Bend; allow 60–90 minutes for parking, the walk, and the overlook.
  • Plan for the current $10 passenger-vehicle parking fee at Horseshoe Bend. The lot is operated by the City of Page, and National Park passes do not cover it. Recheck the fee before departure.
  • Continue to Bryce Canyon City or Tropic and check in. Do not add Antelope Canyon or another Page-area attraction to this transfer day.
  • Stay: Best Western Plus Ruby's Inn. Booked for Nov 24–25 · $96.30.

Horseshoe Bend visit information · NPS parking and pass information

Day 5 — Wednesday, November 25

Bryce Canyon → Springdale

About 2–2.5 hours driving, before park stops.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Bryce Canyon → Springdale

  • Use the morning and early afternoon for Bryce's amphitheater viewpoints, such as Sunrise Point, Sunset Point, and Inspiration Point.
  • If trails are open and not icy, consider Queen's Garden. Switch to rim viewpoints if footing is unsafe; traction devices may help on icy paths.
  • Drive to Springdale via UT-12, US-89, and UT-9. Check road conditions before leaving Bryce.
  • Stay: Bumbleberry Inn. Booked for Nov 25–26 · $206.91. Booking website ↗

Bryce winter conditions

Day 6 — Thursday, November 26 · Thanksgiving

Springdale → Zion National Park → Las Vegas

Spend the morning and early afternoon in Zion, then allow about 2.5–3 hours to drive to Las Vegas.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Zion National Park · Thanksgiving

  • Hike Canyon Overlook Trail and Lower Emerald Pool Trail in the morning. Allow time to travel between trailheads, then leave Zion in the early afternoon for Las Vegas.
  • The NPS schedule currently lists Zion Canyon shuttle service from Oct 25 through Nov 28: first shuttle at 7 AM, last from the Visitor Center at 5 PM, and last out of the canyon at 6:15 PM. The schedule can change; confirm it close to the trip.
  • Thanksgiving dining options may be limited. Reserve dinner ahead or bring food and snacks.
  • Leave Zion in the afternoon and drive about 2.5–3 hours to Las Vegas.
  • Stay: Virgin Hotels Las Vegas, Curio Collection by Hilton. Booked for Nov 26–27 · $127.71.

Current Zion shuttle schedule

Day 7 — Friday, November 27

Las Vegas → Harry Reid International Airport

Allow extra time for the hotel-to-airport drive, rental-car return, and check-in.

Open in Google Maps ↗

Las Vegas → home

  • For a flight at 3 PM or later, leave your Las Vegas hotel with extra time for the airport drive, fuel, rental return, and check-in.
  • Refuel according to the rental agreement, return the car at LAS, and fly home from Las Vegas.

Preparation checklist

  • Book a Las Vegas return flight for Nov 27 at 3 PM or later.
  • Recheck Bryce road, trail, and weather conditions before driving; snow can temporarily close the upper park road.
  • Confirm Zion shuttle hours for the Nov 26 half day and Thanksgiving dining availability.
  • Bring warm layers, gloves, a beanie, rain shell, and grippy footwear; carry water, snacks, and offline maps.
  • Check current Monument Valley hours and tour availability if you want a guided visit.
